A note from The Gathering Place

Welcome to the Table

Welcome to The Gathering Place! This is a space for us to grow in God’s Word, encourage one another, and build genuine connections beyond our weekly Bible study.

Each day, take a moment to reflect on what you’re grateful for. It may be something big, something small, or simply a reminder of God’s faithfulness. You’re welcome to share your thoughts, add a photo, read what others have shared, or leave a word of encouragement.

You don’t have to have everything figured out to be part of this community. Come ready to learn, grow, and show kindness to one another.
