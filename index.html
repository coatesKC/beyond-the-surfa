TOGETHER WE BRING MORE

One Community. Many Gifts.

Beyond the Surface is a community of women bringing together faith, experience, resources, talents, and opportunities to support one another and serve others.

Some of us teach. Some coach. Some speak. Some serve. Some create. Some lead groups. Some simply show up with encouragement, prayer, and a willing heart.

WHY BEYOND THE SURFACE?

The Message Behind the Logo

The anatomical heart represents a heart completely surrendered to God. The thin gold lines represent something precious being brought into the light.

Beyond — we are going deeper.
The — there is something beneath what is immediately visible.
Surface — what people see is not the whole story.

Beyond the Surface: where the hidden places become an offering.
